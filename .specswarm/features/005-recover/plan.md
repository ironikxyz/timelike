## Constitution Check

- **H1 (boundary):** agent-side only; nothing reaches beyond the environment, and Adele is not involved.
- **H2 (one output contract):** both commands use agentio. `undo` is mutating and destructive, so it has
  `--yes` and `--dry-run` and uses the confirmation envelope (exit 4).
- **H3 (verify artifacts, not messages):** `undo --yes` says "restored" only after re-walking the
  workspace and finding no difference from the snapshot (FR-19). The tests compare contents themselves.
- **H4 (non-interactive):** no prompt or terminal read; the lock waits a bounded time and then fails
  with a verdict.
- **H5 (stdlib, pinned interpreter):** two single files with the image shebang; no third-party
  dependency. `undo` loads `snapshot` from its own directory (R8).
- **H6 (no hidden machinery):** shell commands, no daemon; nothing runs between invocations.
- **H7 (every criterion a test):** SC-1 to SC-4 each get one e2e file named after their text, plus
  units; SC-5 (D7) is Manual and stays unconfirmed until observed.

- **T2 (P5 vs P2):** the caps and their verdicts (FR-6 to FR-8).
- **Revision 8's git-state boundary:** `.git` is out of scope at every depth, and the write and remove
  helpers refuse it themselves (D-4, R3).
