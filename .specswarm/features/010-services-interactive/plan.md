## Summary

One new agent tool, **`tools/bin/services`**, with four subcommands: `start`, `stop`, `list` and `logs`.

- `start` launches a command in its own session, with an inherited marker. It waits for port or log
  readiness and concludes ready, died, or not ready (spec FR-1 to FR-4).
- `stop` ends the whole tree, found by marker, log writers and group, with `run`'s sweep, and re-checks
  (FR-5 to FR-7).
- `list` and `logs` read the registry, a file in the session scratch (FR-8, FR-9).
- Rule 9 follows revision 13: `start` and own `stop` are not confirmed; `stop --session` and `--all` are.
