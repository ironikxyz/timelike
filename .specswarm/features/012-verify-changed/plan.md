## Summary

One new agent tool, **`tools/bin/verify`**, with two subcommands (spec FR-1 to FR-14):
- **`test -- CMD`** runs CMD through `run` and reads `run`'s log. It reports the counts and the failures
  with their locations, for pytest, jest, vitest, go test and cargo test. Otherwise it reports
  `format unknown`. The exit passes through.
- **`changed`** finds the working changes with git. It selects:
  - the tests importing them, through `symbols dependents` (011 at `6d523d4`);
  - lint and type-check of the changed files only.

  It runs each step through `run`, and states what it selected and why.

**The parsers are tested on output recorded from the real runners** (seam 1, R1). In the image, real
pytest, ruff and mypy come from the pinned uv, as the lane's own steps get them. **No tool is added to the
image.**
